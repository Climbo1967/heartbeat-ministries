date: 2026-06-30
text: I lift up my eyes to the mountains— where does my help come from? My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.
reference: Psalm 121:1-2
translation: NIV
devotional: By Tuesday the week is fully underway, and it's easy to keep our eyes fixed on the climb in front of us — the deadlines, the demands, the mountains that seem to grow taller the longer we stare at them. The psalmist looks up at those same heights and asks the honest question we all carry: where does my help come from? The answer steadies everything — not our own strength, not the hills themselves, but the LORD who made heaven and earth. The same God who hung the stars is paying attention to your small, ordinary today. Lift your eyes from the size of the task to the size of the One who helps you, and let that reframe the whole week.
prayer: Father, when the week feels like an uphill climb, lift my eyes to You. Remind me that my help comes not from my own effort but from Your hand, the Maker of heaven and earth. Steady me today, and help me walk through whatever is ahead knowing You are watching over every step. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
