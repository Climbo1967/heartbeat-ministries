date: 2026-07-30
text: For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do.
reference: Ephesians 2:10
translation: NIV
devotional: The word Paul uses for "handiwork" is the same root we get "poem" from—you are God's craftsmanship, not an accident and not a rough draft. That alone should settle the question of your worth on a Thursday when the week feels ordinary. But notice He did not stop at making you; He prepared work in advance for you to walk into. The kindness you offer today, the phone call you make, the job you do faithfully when no one is watching—those were laid out ahead of time by a God who wanted you specifically to do them. Ordinary days are where prepared works usually hide.
prayer: Father, thank You for making me on purpose and for the work You have already prepared for me today. Open my eyes to see it—the small kindnesses, the honest labor, the person who needs an encouraging word. Let me walk into it gladly, knowing it came from Your hand. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
