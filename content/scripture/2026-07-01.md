date: 2026-07-01
text: Forget the former things; do not dwell on the past. See, I am doing a new thing! Now it springs up; do you not perceive it? I am making a way in the wilderness and streams in the wasteland.
reference: Isaiah 43:18-19
translation: NIV
devotional: Today is more than a Wednesday — it's the first page of a brand-new month, and God meets us here with a promise. He tells a weary people not to stay chained to what's behind them, because He is already at work doing something new. Notice the tense: not "I will someday," but "now it springs up" — the new thing is already breaking through the ground of your ordinary day, even when you can't yet see it. Wherever this year has felt like a wilderness, He is the One who carves paths through it and sends streams into the dry places. Step into July with expectant eyes, trusting that the God who makes ways is making one for you.
prayer: Father, thank You that You are always doing something new. As this month begins, help me to release what lies behind and to trust You with what's ahead. Open my eyes to see the ways You are already making and the streams You are bringing into my dry places. Lead me forward in hope today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
