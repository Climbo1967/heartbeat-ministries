text: being confident of this, that he who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.
reference: Philippians 1:6
translation: NIV
devotional: When a new week begins, it's easy to fix our eyes on everything still unfinished — in our work, our relationships, and our own hearts. Paul reminds us that the most important work of all is one we didn't start and can't finish on our own: God Himself began a good work in you, and He has promised to see it through. You are not a project you must complete by sheer effort; you are someone God is faithfully shaping, day by day, all the way to the end. So step into this Monday with confidence — not in your own strength, but in the steady, patient hand of the One who never abandons what He begins.
prayer: Father, thank You for beginning a good work in me and for promising to complete it. When I feel unfinished or discouraged, remind me that You are faithful and never give up on me. Help me walk into this new week trusting Your steady hand. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
