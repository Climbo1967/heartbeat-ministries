text: Though the mountains be shaken and the hills be removed, yet my unfailing love for you will not be shaken nor my covenant of peace be removed, says the LORD, who has compassion on you.
reference: Isaiah 54:10
translation: NIV
devotional: Everything around us can change — health, finances, even the people we lean on. God points to the most permanent things we know, mountains and hills, and says His love is more lasting still. Whatever is shifting under your feet today, His covenant of peace stands unmoved. Rest your weight on what cannot be shaken.
prayer: Lord, when life feels unsteady, remind me that Your love never moves. Help me rest in Your peace today and trust Your compassion to carry me through whatever changes come. Amen.
