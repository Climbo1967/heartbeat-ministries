text: Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.
reference: Proverbs 3:5-6
translation: NIV
devotional: There's a quiet relief in these words: you don't have to have it all figured out. On a Saturday, when the week's loose ends and unanswered questions tend to surface, this verse invites you to loosen your grip. Trusting God with all your heart doesn't mean understanding every turn of the road — it means handing Him the parts you can't see. As you bring your ways to Him, He promises to do what you cannot: straighten the path ahead, step by step.
prayer: Father, I don't understand everything in front of me, and I'm tired of trying to carry it all on my own reasoning. Today I choose to trust You with my whole heart. Guide my steps, straighten my path, and help me rest in the truth that You see what I cannot. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
