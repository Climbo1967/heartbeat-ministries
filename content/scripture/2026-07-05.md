text: And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.
reference: Romans 8:28
translation: NIV
devotional: "All things" is a big promise — not just the good days, but the setbacks, the delays, and the moments that make no sense at the time. Paul doesn't say all things are good; he says God works in all things for good. That's the difference between a life of random events and a life held by purpose. Whatever this Sunday brings, nothing that touches you is wasted in His hands. He is weaving even today's loose threads into something good.
prayer: Father, thank You that nothing in my life is outside Your purpose. When I can't see how the pieces fit, help me trust that You are working all things for good. Give me peace in the waiting. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
