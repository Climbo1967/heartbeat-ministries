text: Let the morning bring me word of your unfailing love, for I have put my trust in you. Show me the way I should go, for to you I entrust my life.
reference: Psalm 143:8
translation: NIV
devotional: David wrote this prayer while hemmed in by trouble, yet his first request each morning wasn't for rescue—it was to hear of God's unfailing love. That order matters. Before the day tells you what it demands, let God tell you who He is and how He loves you. Then the second request follows naturally—show me the way I should go. When your day starts anchored in His love, guidance and trust come easier, because you already know the heart of the One leading you.
prayer: Father, before this day gets loud, let me hear of Your unfailing love. I put my trust in You—show me the way I should go today, and I will entrust my whole life to You, step by step. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
