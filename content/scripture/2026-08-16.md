text: Come, let us sing for joy to the LORD; let us shout aloud to the Rock of our salvation. Let us come before him with thanksgiving and extol him with music and song.
reference: Psalm 95:1-2
translation: NIV
devotional: The psalmist doesn't suggest worship — he invites us into it, like a friend pulling us toward something too good to keep quiet about. Notice that the singing comes before anything else. Not once problems are solved, not once we feel ready, but first. On this Lord's Day, thanksgiving is the doorway in, and the Rock of our salvation is the reason we can enter boldly. Whatever this week held, He has been steady beneath you the whole time. That alone is worth a song.
prayer: Lord, You are the Rock of my salvation, and today I choose to come to You with joy. Tune my heart to sing Your praise, and let thanksgiving lead me into Your presence this day and all week long. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
