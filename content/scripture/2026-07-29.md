date: 2026-07-29
text: Praise the LORD, my soul, and forget not all his benefits—who forgives all your sins and heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from the pit and crowns you with love and compassion.
reference: Psalm 103:2-4
translation: NIV
devotional: David is preaching to himself here, and that is worth noticing. He tells his own soul to remember, because forgetting is the easier thing to do. By Wednesday the week has usually piled up enough noise that yesterday's mercies get buried under today's demands. So stop and count them. The same God who forgives what you cannot undo is the God who lifts you out of the pit you could not climb out of, and He does not do it grudgingly—He crowns you with love and compassion. You are not merely tolerated by Him. You are treasured.
prayer: Father, forgive me for how quickly I forget what You have already done. Today I choose to remember—Your forgiveness, Your healing, Your rescue, Your steady love that has never once let go of me. Let gratitude reset my heart and carry me through the rest of this week. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
