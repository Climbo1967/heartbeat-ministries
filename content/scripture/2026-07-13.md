date: 2026-07-13
text: May the favor of the Lord our God rest on us; establish the work of our hands for us—yes, establish the work of our hands.
reference: Psalm 90:17
translation: NIV
devotional: As Monday opens a new week, this closing prayer of Moses is a fitting place to begin. Notice that he doesn't ask for an easier week or lighter work—he asks that God's favor would rest on ordinary work and that God Himself would establish it. The things you'll put your hands to this week—your job, your home, the small unseen tasks—all matter to God, and He delights to give them lasting purpose. You don't have to carry this week in your own strength; invite Him into the work and let Him make it count. Whatever is on your list today, offer it to Him and ask Him to establish it.
prayer: Father, as this new week begins, let Your favor rest on me and on all that I set my hands to do. I don't want to labor in my own strength—establish the work of my hands and give it purpose that lasts. Use even the ordinary parts of my week for Your glory. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
