text: Cast your cares on the LORD and he will sustain you; he will never let the righteous be shaken.
reference: Psalm 55:22
translation: NIV
devotional: Whatever you are carrying today, God never intended for you to carry it alone. The invitation here is simple — hand it over. Casting our cares on the Lord is not a one-time act but a daily habit of trust, and His promise in return is steady, sustaining grace. You may feel the weight, but you will not be shaken, because the One who holds you cannot be moved.
prayer: Father, I bring You every worry and burden I have been carrying. Teach me to release them into Your hands and to trust Your sustaining strength today. Hold me steady when I feel shaken, and let my heart rest in You. Amen.
