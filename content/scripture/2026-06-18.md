text: The LORD your God is in your midst, a mighty one who will save; he will rejoice over you with gladness; he will quiet you by his love; he will exult over you with loud singing.
reference: Zephaniah 3:17
translation: ESV
devotional: We often picture God as patient or just — but here He is pictured singing. Not over a finished, polished version of you, but over you as you are right now. The same God who is mighty enough to save is tender enough to quiet a restless heart with His love. If you've spent the day feeling unseen or unworthy of delight, let this reframe it: you are not merely tolerated by heaven, you are rejoiced over. Sit with that today and let it settle the noise inside you.
prayer: Father, it's hard to imagine that You delight in me, yet Your Word says You do. Quiet the anxious places in my heart with Your love today, and help me rest in the truth that I am Yours and You are glad. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
