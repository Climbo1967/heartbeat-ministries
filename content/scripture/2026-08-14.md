date: 2026-08-14
text: For his anger lasts only a moment, but his favor lasts a lifetime; weeping may stay for the night, but rejoicing comes in the morning.
reference: Psalm 30:5
translation: NIV
devotional: David knew what it was to lie awake with a heavy heart. He doesn't pretend the night isn't long or that the tears aren't real. He simply insists they don't get the last word. God's favor outlasts every hard season, and morning always comes for the one who belongs to Him. If you're still in the night, hold on. Joy is not a maybe; it's an appointment God has already set.
prayer: Father, thank You that Your favor over my life is longer than any sorrow I carry. Meet me in the night watches and remind me morning is coming. Give me grace to wait on You with hope. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
