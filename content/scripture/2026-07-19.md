date: 2026-07-19
text: I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the LORD.
reference: Psalm 122:1
translation: KJV
devotional: David's joy wasn't in the building—it was in the invitation. Gathering with God's people to worship is not an obligation to endure but a gift to receive, a foretaste of the day when every tribe and tongue will worship together. If you're able to gather with believers today, go gladly; if you can't, know that the Lord meets you right where you are, and your worship is no less precious to Him.
prayer: Lord, give me a glad heart for worship today. Thank You that You invite me into Your presence—not because I've earned it, but because Jesus opened the way. Let my praise be joyful and my heart be near to You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
