date: 2026-06-16
text: It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
reference: Lamentations 3:22-23
translation: KJV
devotional: These words were written in the middle of ruin — Jeremiah looking out over a broken city. And right there, in the rubble, he remembered something steadier than his circumstances: God's mercy doesn't run dry. It isn't rationed out from yesterday's supply. Every single morning, His compassion arrives fresh, like manna. Whatever yesterday cost you, today comes with a new measure of grace. You don't have to carry the weight of the old day into this one.
prayer: Father, thank You that Your mercies are new this morning — that I get to begin again in Your faithfulness. Where I feel worn down or used up, fill me with the fresh grace only You can give. Help me to trust that Your compassion will meet me again tomorrow, just as it has today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
