date: 2026-07-18
text: For the Spirit God gave us does not make us timid, but gives us power, love and self-discipline.
reference: 2 Timothy 1:7
translation: NIV
devotional: Paul wrote these words to encourage a young pastor who was tempted to shrink back. Fear will always try to make us smaller than God made us to be—quieter, more hesitant, more self-protective. But the Spirit living in you is not a spirit of timidity; He supplies power for the task, love for the people in front of you, and the steady self-discipline to keep going when feelings say stop. Whatever you're facing today, you don't have to summon courage from thin air—you already carry the Spirit who gives it.
prayer: Father, thank You that Your Spirit in me is not one of fear but of power, love, and a sound mind. Where I feel timid today, fill me with holy boldness; where I feel weak, be my strength; and where I am tempted to pull back, help me love and press on. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
