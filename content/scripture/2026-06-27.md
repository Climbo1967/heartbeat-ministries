date: 2026-06-27
text: Be still, and know that I am God.
reference: Psalm 46:10
translation: NIV
devotional: This short command carries a deep invitation: before it tells us anything about ourselves, it points us back to God. To "be still" isn't about doing nothing — it's about loosening our grip on the things we've been straining to control and remembering who actually holds them. The verse sits in the middle of a psalm about mountains shaking and waters roaring, which means stillness isn't reserved for calm seasons; it's offered right in the storm. As Saturday gives you a pause in the week, let it be more than rest for your body — let your soul grow quiet long enough to remember that He is God, and you don't carry any of it alone. He stays steady when everything else feels shaky.
prayer: Father, quiet the noise within me and help me to be still in Your presence. When life feels like it is spinning, remind me that You are God and You are in control. Thank You that I can rest in You today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
