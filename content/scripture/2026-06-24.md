text: Let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.
reference: Galatians 6:9
translation: NIV
devotional: Midweek has a way of wearing us thin. The good things you set out to do on Monday — the patience, the kindness, the quiet faithfulness no one notices — can start to feel like they're going nowhere by Wednesday. Paul's words are for exactly this moment: keep going. The harvest he promises doesn't always arrive on our schedule, but it does come, at the proper time. Your steady, unseen good is never wasted in God's economy, so don't give up on it today.
prayer: Father, when I grow tired of doing what is right, renew my strength and steady my heart. Help me keep sowing good seed today, trusting that You will bring the harvest in Your perfect time. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
