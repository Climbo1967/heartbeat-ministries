text: The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing. He makes me lie down in green pastures, he leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul. He guides me along the right paths for his name's sake.
reference: Psalm 23:1-3
translation: NIV
devotional: There is deep comfort in calling God our Shepherd — it means we are never wandering alone and never responsible for finding the way by ourselves. He leads to green pastures and still waters, places of provision and peace, and He does it not because we have earned it but simply because of who He is. Notice that God doesn't merely point us toward rest; He makes us lie down, gently insisting on the renewal we so often rush past. On this Sunday, let Him refresh your soul — set down the week behind you and the week ahead, and trust the One who is still guiding you along the right paths. You lack nothing today, because the Shepherd Himself is with you.
prayer: Father, thank You for being my Shepherd, the One who leads, provides, and restores. Quiet my heart today and let me rest in Your care, trusting that You are guiding my every step. Refresh my soul and help me to follow where You lead. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
