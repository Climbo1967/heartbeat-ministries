text: Whoever dwells in the shelter of the Most High will rest in the shadow of the Almighty. I will say of the Lord, "He is my refuge and my fortress, my God, in whom I trust."
reference: Psalm 91:1-2
translation: NIV
devotional: To dwell in the shelter of the Most High is not a one-time visit but a daily choosing to make God our home. The psalmist never promises a life without storms; he promises a refuge that holds firm in the middle of them, a shadow of the Almighty where fear loses its grip. Notice that the shelter becomes real the moment we say it out loud, declaring over our own hearts that the Lord is our refuge and our fortress. Wherever this Wednesday finds you, you are invited to step under His covering and rest, trusting the God who has never once failed those who hide themselves in Him.
prayer: Father, thank You that You are my refuge and my fortress, a shelter that never gives way. When I am afraid, teach me to run to You first and to rest in the shadow of Your wings. Help me to trust You with everything I am carrying today. In Jesus' Name I pray, Amen.
