date: 2026-08-18
text: When anxiety was great within me, your consolation brought me joy.
reference: Psalm 94:19
translation: NIV
devotional: The psalmist doesn't pretend his anxiety was small — he calls it great, a crowd of worries pressing in at once. Yet he discovered that God's comfort was greater still, meeting him right in the middle of the noise. Consolation isn't a distraction from what troubles you; it's the nearness of a God who sits with you in it until joy returns. Whatever is stacking up inside you this Tuesday, bring it to Him honestly. He is not overwhelmed by what overwhelms you.
prayer: Father, You see every anxious thought crowding my mind today. Meet me in the middle of them with Your comfort, and turn my worry into joy as I trust You with what I cannot control. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
