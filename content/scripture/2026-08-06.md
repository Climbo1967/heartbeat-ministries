text: The Lord makes firm the steps of the one who delights in him; though he may stumble, he will not fall, for the Lord upholds him with his hand.
reference: Psalm 37:23-24
translation: NIV
devotional: There is a difference between stumbling and falling, and God knows it well. A stumble is not the end of the walk—it is simply a moment when the ground shifts and the Lord's hand tightens its grip. David wrote this as a man who had stumbled plenty, yet never once slipped out of God's hold. If this Thursday has you unsteady, remember that the same God who orders your steps is the One holding you up. He does not let go when you wobble.
prayer: Father, thank You for ordering my steps and holding me steady when I stumble. Keep my delight in You, strengthen what feels shaky today, and remind me that Your hand is stronger than my missteps. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
