text: The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in him, and he helps me. My heart leaps for joy, and with my song I praise him.
reference: Psalm 28:7
translation: NIV
devotional: By Wednesday the demands of the week are in full swing, and it's a good moment to remember where your strength really comes from. Notice how David stacks it up—the Lord is his strength and his shield, the One he trusts and the One who actually helps. Strength to keep going, protection for the battles, and help in the very moment all flow from the same place—a heart that leans on God. And look where it leads—not to grim endurance, but to a heart that leaps for joy and breaks into song. However far you are into your week, trust Him afresh today and let Him be both your strength and your reason to sing.
prayer: Father, You are my strength and my shield, and my heart trusts in You. Thank You for helping me in the very things that feel too heavy to carry on my own. Fill my heart with joy today until praise rises up in me, even in the middle of an ordinary week. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
