date: 2026-07-14
text: You will keep in perfect peace those whose minds are steadfast, because they trust in you.
reference: Isaiah 26:3
translation: NIV
devotional: By Tuesday the week is well underway, and the noise of it can crowd out our peace. Notice what Isaiah promises—not the absence of trouble, but perfect peace for the mind that stays fixed on God. To be steadfast is to lean hard on the Lord and refuse to be pulled in a hundred directions. Peace, then, isn't something we manufacture; it's what God keeps for us as we keep trusting Him. Whatever is competing for your attention today, turn your thoughts toward Him and let Him guard your heart with a peace the day can't shake.
prayer: Father, in the middle of a busy week I choose to fix my mind on You. Quiet the noise and the worry, and keep me in the perfect peace You promise to those who trust You. Help me lean on You today instead of my own understanding. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
