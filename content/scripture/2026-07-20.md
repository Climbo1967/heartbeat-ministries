date: 2026-07-20
text: Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.
reference: Psalm 119:105
translation: KJV
devotional: We rarely get to see the whole road ahead—and God, in His wisdom, doesn't promise us that. What He promises is light for the next step. A lamp at your feet won't show you next year, but it will keep you from stumbling today. Open His Word this morning and let it guide the one step in front of you; He will handle the miles beyond.
prayer: Father, thank You for Your Word that lights my path. When the way ahead feels dark or uncertain, help me trust You for the next step and walk in the light You've already given. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
