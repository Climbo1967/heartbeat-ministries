text: Do not grieve, for the joy of the LORD is your strength.
reference: Nehemiah 8:10
translation: NIV
devotional: These words were spoken to people who had just rediscovered God's Word after years of loss, and their first response was to weep. But Nehemiah lifts their eyes: the day is holy, and there is reason for joy. Notice that the joy here isn't something you summon up on your own — it's the joy of the LORD, His gladness becoming the very thing that holds you up. By Thursday the week can leave you running on empty, but your strength was never meant to come from gritting your teeth and pushing harder. Let His joy be the fuel today, and lean on the gladness that comes from belonging to Him.
prayer: Father, when I feel drained and worn thin, remind me that my strength comes from You. Fill me with the joy that only You can give, and let it steady me through the rest of this week. Help me to find my gladness in belonging to You today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
