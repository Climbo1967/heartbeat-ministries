text: Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you.
reference: 1 Peter 5:7
translation: NIV
devotional: What a tender invitation this is — not to manage our worries better, but to hand them over entirely. Peter doesn't say cast some of your anxiety, but all of it, because every burden you carry matters to the God who made you. The reason we can let go is not that our problems are small, but that His care for us is personal and constant. Whatever is weighing on you this Thursday morning, He is not too busy for it. Release it into hands far stronger than your own.
prayer: Father, I bring You every worry I've been carrying — the ones I speak about and the ones I hide. Thank You that You care for me personally. Help me to truly release them to You today and to rest in Your faithful love. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
