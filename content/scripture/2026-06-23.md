---
date: 2026-06-23
text: My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.
reference: 2 Corinthians 12:9
translation: NIV
devotional: Paul wrote these words after begging God three times to remove a struggle that wouldn't lift. The answer he got wasn't the one he wanted — but it was better: God's grace would be enough, and His strength would show up most clearly right where Paul felt weakest. The same is true for you. The limitation that frustrates you, the place you feel you're falling short — that's not where God walks away. It's exactly where His power is free to do what your own strength never could. You don't have to be enough today, because His grace already is.
prayer: Father, thank You that I don't have to hide my weakness from You or pretend I have it all together. Where I feel inadequate today, let Your grace be enough, and let Your strength be made perfect in the very places I feel I'm lacking. Help me rest in You instead of striving on my own. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
