text: Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.
reference: Matthew 11:28-29
translation: NIV
devotional: As the week winds down, you may be carrying more than you let on — tiredness, worry, the weight of things left undone. Jesus doesn't ask you to clean yourself up first or push through on your own strength. He simply says, come. The invitation is to trade your heavy load for His easy yoke and to walk beside the One who is gentle with the worn-out and humble with the struggling. Rest isn't something you have to earn this weekend; it's something He offers freely right now.
prayer: Lord Jesus, I come to You today just as I am, tired and carrying more than I can hold. Thank You that You meet the weary with gentleness, not demands. Take my burdens, give my soul Your rest, and teach me to walk close beside You. In Your Name I pray, Amen.
