date: 2026-08-08
text: Yes, my soul, find rest in God; my hope comes from him. Truly he is my rock and my salvation; he is my fortress, I will not be shaken.
reference: Psalm 62:5-6
translation: NIV
devotional: David is not describing a feeling he woke up with. He is talking to himself, telling his own soul where to go looking for rest. Some Saturdays that is exactly the work—reminding a tired heart that its footing was never its own strength in the first place. A rock does not shift when the week has been heavy, and a fortress does not need you to hold it up. Rest today in the One who holds you.
prayer: Father, quiet my soul and settle my hope in You. When I am tempted to build my footing on my own strength, remind me that You are my rock and my fortress. Give me true rest today, and let me rise tomorrow steady in You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
