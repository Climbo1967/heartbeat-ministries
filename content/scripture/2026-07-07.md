text: The Lord is my light and my salvation—whom shall I fear? The Lord is the stronghold of my life—of whom shall I be afraid?
reference: Psalm 27:1
translation: NIV
devotional: David opens this psalm not by pretending danger doesn't exist, but by naming Someone bigger than all of it. When the Lord is your light, no darkness can hide the way forward; when He is your salvation, no threat gets the final word. Notice that David asks a question rather than makes a boast — "whom shall I fear?" — because once you know who holds your life, the things that loomed so large begin to shrink. Whatever you're walking into today, you don't face it alone or unguarded; the Lord Himself is the stronghold around you. Let that truth quiet the fear before it ever takes root.
prayer: Father, You are my light in every uncertainty and my salvation in every trial. When fear rises, remind me that You are the stronghold of my life, and steady my heart to trust You. Help me walk through this day unafraid because You are with me. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
