text: Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God will be with you wherever you go.
reference: Joshua 1:9
translation: NIV
devotional: God gave Joshua these words as he stood at the edge of an unknown future, facing a task far bigger than himself. Notice that courage here isn't the absence of fear — it's a choice rooted in a promise. The command to be strong comes wrapped in a reason: you are not alone. Wherever today takes you, into whatever room or conversation or worry, God goes with you. You don't have to manufacture bravery from nothing; you can draw it from His presence.
prayer: Father, where I feel afraid or discouraged today, remind me that You are with me. Steady my heart, strengthen my steps, and help me walk forward in the courage that comes from knowing You never leave my side. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
