text: For I am convinced that neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither the present nor the future, nor any powers, neither height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
reference: Romans 8:38-39
translation: NIV
devotional: Paul doesn't say he hopes or thinks — he says he is convinced. He lists every force he can imagine, seen and unseen, present and future, and declares that none of it can pry you out of God's love. Whatever is pressing on you today, it didn't make the list of things stronger than His grip. You are held, not because you're holding on well, but because He is.
prayer: Father, thank You that Your love doesn't loosen when life shakes. When I feel far from You, remind me that nothing — not my failures, my fears, or my circumstances — can separate me from You. Help me rest in that today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
