text: Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled and do not be afraid.
reference: John 14:27
translation: NIV
devotional: The world offers peace that depends on circumstances—quiet only when the storm passes. Jesus offers something different, a peace that holds steady while the wind still blows. He spoke these words on the night He was betrayed, proof that His peace is not the absence of trouble but His presence in the middle of it. Whatever is unsettling your heart this Wednesday, He is not rationing His peace. He gives it freely, fully, and personally—to you.
prayer: Lord Jesus, thank You for a peace the world cannot give and cannot take away. Calm my troubled heart, quiet my fears, and let Your presence steady me through everything this day holds. In Your Name, Amen.
