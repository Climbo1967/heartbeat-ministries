---
date: 2026-07-28
text: Let us then approach God's throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need.
reference: Hebrews 4:16
translation: NIV
devotional: Notice the word confidence. We are not told to creep toward God's throne hoping He is in a good mood, but to come boldly, because the One seated there already knows our weakness and welcomes us anyway. Jesus made that approach possible, and He did not make it available only for our best days. Whatever you are carrying into this Tuesday—a worry you have not said out loud, a failure you keep replaying, a need that feels too ordinary to bother God with—that is exactly what grace is for. Come as you are, and come now, in your time of need.
prayer: Father, thank You that the way to You is open because of Jesus. Forgive me for hesitating when You have invited me to come boldly. Meet me today with mercy where I have failed and with grace where I am weak, and help me to bring You everything rather than carrying it alone. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
