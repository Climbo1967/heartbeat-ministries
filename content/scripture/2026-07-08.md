text: Therefore we do not lose heart. Though outwardly we are wasting away, yet inwardly we are being renewed day by day.
reference: 2 Corinthians 4:16
translation: NIV
devotional: By Wednesday the week can start to show on us — energy thinning, patience wearing, the outward self feeling every mile. Paul is honest about that wasting away, yet he refuses to lose heart, because he knows something deeper is happening underneath. While the body tires, God is quietly renewing the inner person day by day, fresh each morning like this one. Whatever this midweek day is costing you on the outside, God is at work on the inside, making you new in the very moment you feel most worn.
prayer: Father, when the middle of the week leaves me weary on the outside, renew me on the inside today. Thank You that You are making me new day by day, even when I can't feel it. Help me not to lose heart, but to trust the quiet work of Your hand. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
