text: I have told you these things, so that in me you may have peace. In this world you will have trouble. But take heart! I have overcome the world.
reference: John 16:33
translation: NIV
devotional: Jesus spoke these words on the night before the cross — He knew exactly what trouble looked like, and He never promised us a life without it. What He promised instead is something far better: peace that lives in Him, not in our circumstances. Notice He doesn't say "try to overcome the world" — He says He already has. Whatever trouble this Friday holds, you face it standing behind a victory already won. Take heart today.
prayer: Lord Jesus, thank You that Your peace doesn't depend on my circumstances. When trouble comes today, remind me that You have already overcome it all. Help me take heart and rest in Your victory. In Your Name, Amen.
