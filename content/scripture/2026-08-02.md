date: 2026-08-02
text: Be joyful in hope, patient in affliction, faithful in prayer.
reference: Romans 12:12
translation: NIV
devotional: In just twelve words, Paul gives us a rhythm for the whole Christian life. Joy is anchored not in our circumstances but in our hope—what God has promised is coming. Patience is possible in affliction because affliction is never the end of the story. And prayer is the thread that holds it all together, keeping us connected to the One who supplies both the joy and the patience. On this Lord's Day, whichever of the three feels hardest right now, He offers the grace for it.
prayer: Father, teach me to rejoice in the hope You have given, to stay steady when trials come, and to keep coming back to You in prayer. Hold me close this Sunday and through the week ahead. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
