text: I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.
reference: Philippians 4:13
translation: KJV
devotional: Paul wrote these words not from a mountaintop of success but from a prison cell, and just before them he said he had learned to be content whether full or hungry, in plenty or in want. This is not a promise that we can achieve anything we set our minds to—it is the deeper assurance that in every circumstance Christ supplies the strength to endure, to serve, and to remain faithful. Whatever you're facing today, you don't face it on your own power. The same Christ who sustained Paul stands ready to strengthen you for exactly what this day requires.
prayer: Lord Jesus, thank You that my strength doesn't have to be enough, because Yours always is. Teach me contentment in every season, and carry me through whatever today holds by Your power at work in me. In Your Name I pray, Amen.
