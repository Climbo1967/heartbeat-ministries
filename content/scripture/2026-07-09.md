text: Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the one who takes refuge in him.
reference: Psalm 34:8
translation: NIV
devotional: Some mornings we wake already bracing for the day, sizing God up from a distance and wondering if He is really enough for what Thursday holds. David gives a gentler invitation — not to figure God out, but to taste and see for ourselves that He is good. Goodness like this is something we come to know by drawing near, by trusting Him with the very thing we are tempted to carry alone. Take refuge in Him today, and you will find He is even better than you hoped.
prayer: Father, thank You that You are good, and that Your goodness is something I can truly taste and see. Today I choose to take refuge in You rather than in my own strength. Open my eyes to Your kindness in the small moments, and let me rest in You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
