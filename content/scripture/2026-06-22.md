text: May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.
reference: Romans 15:13
translation: NIV
devotional: A new week opens in front of you, and it's tempting to meet it bracing for what might go wrong. But notice what this verse points to first — not your effort, but God Himself, the very source of hope. Joy and peace aren't things you have to manufacture before Monday begins; they're what He pours into you as you trust Him. And the goal isn't merely to have just enough to get by, but to overflow — to carry more hope than your circumstances would explain. Let Him fill you this morning before you spend yourself on everything ahead.
prayer: God of hope, as I step into this new week, fill me with Your joy and peace. Where I feel anxious or empty, overflow me with hope by Your Spirit, that I might carry Your light into everyone I meet today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
