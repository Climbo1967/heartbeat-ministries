date: 2026-08-03
text: Commit to the LORD whatever you do, and he will establish your plans.
reference: Proverbs 16:3
translation: NIV
devotional: Monday mornings have a way of handing us a list before we've had a chance to pray over it. Solomon's counsel is simple—before you work the plan, commit the work. To commit means to roll it over onto Him, the deadlines, the decisions, the things you cannot control. God does not promise every plan will go the way we drew it up, but He promises to establish the work of hands that are surrendered to Him. Give Him the week before you spend it.
prayer: Lord, I bring You this day and this week before I begin. Take my work, my plans, and my worries into Your hands, and establish what honors You. Let everything I do today be done for Your glory. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
