date: 2026-07-11
text: You make known to me the path of life; you will fill me with joy in your presence, with eternal pleasures at your right hand.
reference: Psalm 16:11
translation: NIV
devotional: Saturday often comes with a slower pace, and that makes it a good day to remember where real joy is found. David doesn't say God points to the path of life from a distance — He makes it known, walking it with us, and His presence itself is where the fullness of joy lives. The world offers plenty of substitutes, but they run dry; what God gives at His right hand lasts forever. Today, whether your day is full or quiet, take a moment to simply be in His presence and let Him fill you with joy that doesn't depend on circumstances.
prayer: Lord, thank You for making the path of life known to me. Teach me to seek Your presence today above everything else, and fill me with the joy that only comes from being near You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
