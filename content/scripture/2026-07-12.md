text: Enter his gates with thanksgiving and his courts with praise; give thanks to him and praise his name. For the LORD is good and his love endures forever; his faithfulness continues through all generations.
reference: Psalm 100:4-5
translation: NIV
devotional: It's Sunday, and this psalm was written for exactly this — a people gathering to worship. Notice how we're invited to come in — not with requests first, but with thanksgiving and praise. Gratitude is the doorway into God's presence. And the reason is simple — the Lord is good, His love never runs out, and His faithfulness didn't start with us and won't end with us. Whether you're walking into a church building today or worshiping right where you are, start with thanks and watch how it changes everything else.
prayer: Father, I enter Your presence today with a thankful heart. You are good, Your love endures forever, and Your faithfulness reaches every generation, including mine. Fill my worship today with gratitude and praise. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
