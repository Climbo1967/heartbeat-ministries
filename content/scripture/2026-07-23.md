date: 2026-07-23
text: Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come. The old has gone, the new is here!
reference: 2 Corinthians 5:17
translation: NIV
devotional: Whatever yesterday held—its failures, its regrets, the person you fear you will always be—the gospel announces something startling. In Christ, you are not a patched-up version of the old you, but a genuinely new creation. Paul does not say the old is being slowly improved; he says it has gone, and the new has already come. This is not something you must manufacture by trying harder; it is a gift God has already made true of everyone who belongs to Jesus. So step into this Thursday not weighed down by who you used to be, but free to live as the new creation God has already declared you to be.
prayer: Father, thank You that in Christ I am made new—that the old has gone and the new has come by Your grace, not my effort. Where I still cling to old guilt or old habits, help me believe what You have declared true of me and to live as Your new creation today. In Jesus' Name I pray, Amen.
