date: 2026-07-31
text: For the LORD God is a sun and shield; the LORD bestows favor and honor; no good thing does he withhold from those who walk uprightly.
reference: Psalm 84:11
translation: ESV
devotional: A sun and a shield—God gives what we need to grow and guards us while we grow. Most of us are quick to name what we lack at the end of a long month, slower to notice how much has been quietly supplied. The promise here is not that nothing hard will come, but that nothing genuinely good will be withheld from the one walking with Him. If it has not come, it either is not good for you yet or is not good for you at all, and He knows the difference better than you do. Let that settle you as this week closes.
prayer: Father, thank You for being both my light and my defense. Where I have been counting what is missing, teach me to count what You have given. Help me trust that anything truly good will not be held back from me, and give me peace with the things You have said no to. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
