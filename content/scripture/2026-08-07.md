text: The Lord will guide you always; he will satisfy your needs in a sun-scorched land and will strengthen your frame. You will be like a well-watered garden, like a spring whose waters never fail.
reference: Isaiah 58:11
translation: NIV
devotional: God does not promise to keep us out of the sun-scorched places. He promises to be our water in them. Isaiah wrote to people who knew what dry ground looked like, and the picture he gave them was not escape but supply—a garden that stays green because the spring feeding it never runs dry. Whatever has drained you this week, the source you are drawing from is not your own reserves. As you head into the weekend, let Him guide and let Him refill.
prayer: Father, You know the dry places in my life and You have not left me there alone. Guide my steps today, strengthen what has grown weary, and let Your life flow through me like water that never fails. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
