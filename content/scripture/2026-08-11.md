text: The Lord your God is with you, the Mighty Warrior who saves. He will take great delight in you; in his love he will no longer rebuke you, but will rejoice over you with singing.
reference: Zephaniah 3:17
translation: NIV
devotional: It's easy to picture God as a distant judge, tallying up our failures. This verse flips that picture entirely — He's described as delighting in you, singing over you. Not tolerating you. Rejoicing over you. Whatever you're carrying into today, you're not approaching a God who's keeping score; you're walking with One who is glad you're His.
prayer: Lord, thank You that You delight in me, not because I've earned it, but because I am Yours. Quiet my anxious thoughts with the truth that You are near and You are for me. Help me walk today in the confidence of Your love. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
