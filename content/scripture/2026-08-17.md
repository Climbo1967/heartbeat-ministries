text: Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God will be with you wherever you go.
reference: Joshua 1:9
translation: NIV
devotional: God spoke these words to Joshua on the edge of an unknown week of his own — a new land, a new role, and every reason to feel unqualified. Notice that the command to be courageous rests entirely on a promise, that God Himself goes with him. Courage isn't the absence of fear; it's confidence in the One who walks ahead of you. As this new week opens, whatever assignment feels too big for you is not too big for the God who is already there.
prayer: Father, thank You that I don't step into this week alone. When I feel afraid or discouraged, remind me that You are with me wherever I go, and give me the courage to move forward trusting You. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
