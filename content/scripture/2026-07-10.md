devotional: As Friday arrives and a full week settles behind you, these ancient words are still God's heart spoken over you. This was the blessing God gave Aaron to speak over His people — not merely a wish, but a promise of His nearness: to keep you, to shine on you, to turn His face toward you. Notice how personal it is; the God of all creation bends His attention to you individually and offers not just His help but His peace. Whatever this day and the coming weekend hold, receive this blessing as truly yours, and carry His peace with you wherever you go.
prayer: Father, thank You for turning Your face toward me and calling me Your own. As I step into this day, let me know Your keeping, Your grace, and Your peace in a way I can feel. Bless those I love and make Your face shine on us this weekend. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
