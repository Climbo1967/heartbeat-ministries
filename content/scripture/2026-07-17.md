text: Rejoice always, pray continually, give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.
reference: 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18
translation: NIV
devotional: Notice Paul doesn't say give thanks for all circumstances, but in all of them. Joy, prayer, and gratitude aren't feelings we wait on—they're choices we make right in the middle of an ordinary or hard day. Rejoicing keeps our eyes on God rather than our troubles, unceasing prayer keeps the line open, and thanksgiving reminds us that His goodness never left the room. Start small today: one word of praise, one honest prayer, one thing you're grateful for. This is God's will for you, and He supplies the grace to live it.
prayer: Father, teach me to rejoice always, to pray without stopping, and to give thanks in every circumstance. When the day is hard, steady my heart with Your goodness; when it is good, keep me grateful. I trust this is Your will for me, and I ask for grace to live it today. In Jesus' Name, Amen.
